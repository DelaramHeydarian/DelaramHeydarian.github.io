📄 Research

Working Papers / Research Projects

  • Checkout Externalities: A Slow Server Problem with Two Classes
    This project studies routing decisions in a service system with heterogeneous servers and heterogeneous customer classes. I characterize customer equilibrium behavior and compare decentralized routing decisions with the social planner's solution.
  • Customer Routing in Heterogeneous Service Systems
    Research on Wardrop equilibria, socially optimal routing, and the impact of service-time heterogeneity on congestion and customer waiting times.

My research uses analytical models together with computational and simulation methods to understand the operational consequences of customer choice in service systems.
